VINYL EXCHANGE, OLDHAM STREET / DALE STREET, MANCHESTER

Detailed untextured architectural reconstruction for Zealot of Harperhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Vinyl Exchange
on the corner of Oldham Street and Dale Street, Manchester, using ALL
supplied reference images.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

This building must not be treated as a generic record shop.

Its identity depends on the contrast between:

-   the ornate historic upper facade;

-   the curved/corner architecture;

-   repeated arched windows;

-   decorative stone columns and capitals;

-   the modern grey Vinyl Exchange fascia;

-   prominent red signage;

-   aluminium/metal shopfront framing;

-   vents and grille systems;

-   shutters;

-   the Oldham Street and Dale Street street-name plaques.

This pass is primarily for GEOMETRY AND ARCHITECTURAL FORM.

Do not create final textures, album-cover artwork, graffiti, dirt,
stickers, final glass reflections or detailed shop stock yet.

4.  OVERALL MASSING

Begin with the full corner volume.

The building should clearly turn from Oldham Street into Dale Street.

Preserve:

-   the corner angle/curve;

-   projecting corner bay;

-   continuity of upper-storey arches around the corner;

-   long Dale Street elevation;

-   shorter Oldham Street-facing shop entrance elevation;

-   real ground-floor shop depth.

Do not build this as two unrelated flat facades meeting at a simple
90-degree seam.

⸻

5.  HISTORIC UPPER FACADE

THIS IS A HERO ARCHITECTURAL ELEMENT.

The upper level has a repeating sequence of tall arched openings and
decorative vertical stone elements.

Model:

-   real arch depth;

-   thick window reveals;

-   projecting arch mouldings;

-   substantial vertical piers/pilasters;

-   decorative capitals;

-   horizontal decorative bands;

-   recessed spandrel areas;

-   corner projections.

These must cast real shadows.

7.  DECORATIVE CAPITALS

clustered/floral ornament.

Build simplified three-dimensional capitals that preserve:
