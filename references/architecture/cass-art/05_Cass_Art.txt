CASS ART, NORTHERN QUARTER, MANCHESTER

Untextured Blender reconstruction for Zealot of Harperhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Cass Art in the
Northern Quarter, Manchester, using ALL supplied reference photographs.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

This task is GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do not create final textures, decals, logos, window graphics, posters,
product packaging or lighting yet.

The goal is to reconstruct the shop exterior and enough of the interior
architecture that the location is recognisable from its proportions and
physical layout alone, even in neutral clay materials.

⸻

1.  CORE IDENTITY

This Cass Art location has an important visual feature:

The main shop fascia does NOT primarily say “Cass Art”.

Instead, the long orange lettering across the dark grey facade reads:

LET’S FILL THIS TOWN WITH ARTISTS

This wording must be preserved in the later signage/texture pass.

A smaller Cass Art logo/sign appears separately on the facade.

For this geometry stage:

do not model the lettering as final geometry;

do not create final logo textures;

create clean sign/decal surfaces and physical sign housings where
required;

preserve the exact physical placement and proportions needed for the
later signage.

⸻
