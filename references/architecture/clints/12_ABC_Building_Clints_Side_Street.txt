ABC BUILDING, CLINTS + SIDE STREET, MANCHESTER

Full exterior architectural asset with hero Clints storefront

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of the ABC
Buildings complex in Manchester, based on ALL supplied reference
photographs.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

The building must include the complete recognisable external massing of
the ABC building, including its tall tower and lower retail podium.

However, there are two especially important ground-floor locations:

1.  CLINTS

This is the primary hero location.

The player will eventually be able to enter the shop.

2.  SIDE STREET

This needs an accurate and attractive exterior facade and enough
interior depth to look convincing through the glazing, but a detailed
interior is NOT required during this iteration.

The detailed Clints interior will also be built during a later
iteration.

This task is therefore:

DETAILED EXTERIOR GEOMETRY + INTERIOR-READY SHELLS ONLY.

Do not create final textures, clothing, retail stock, posters, graffiti,
plant foliage, logos, final lighting or detailed interior furnishings
yet.

4.  OVERALL BUILDING MASSING

Begin with the complete overall silhouette.

The references show a substantial modernist/post-war office building
comprising:

-   tall rectangular tower;

-   strong repetitive grid facade;

-   lower horizontally extended podium;

-   large retail frontage at ground level;

-   large glazed windows above the retail frontage;

-   a projecting canopy;

-   a contrasting vertical glazed tower/corner section.

Establish these proportions before modelling storefront detail.

The tower should feel tall and imposing when viewed from pavement level.

⸻

5.  TOWER

Model the main tower as efficient modular architecture.

Do not manually build every identical window independently.

Create reusable modules for:

ABC_TowerWindow_Module

ABC_TowerBay_Module

Build the facade from repeated instances or linked geometry.

Preserve:

-   strong rectangular concrete/stone structural grid;

-   recessed glazing;

-   horizontal bands within windows;

-   vertical bay rhythm;

-   large uninterrupted side wall where shown;

-   overall tower proportions.

⸻

6.  TOWER WINDOW GRID

The repeated square/rectangular window grid is the dominant
upper-building feature.

It must read correctly in silhouette and perspective.

Model real recess depth between:

-   projecting structural frame;

-   window plane.

Avoid using a completely flat facade with a window texture.

But do not over-model individual window mechanisms.

⸻

7.  TOWER SIDE WALL

are more solid.

54. SUCCESS CRITERION

The asset succeeds if:

from distance: it unmistakably reads as the large ABC building through
its tower proportions and repetitive window grid;

from street level: the podium and canopy feel architecturally
convincing;

up close: Clints feels like a specific real shop rather than a generic
glass retail unit;

for gameplay: the Clints doorway and interior shell already permit
eventual player entry;

for Side Street: the facade is visually finished enough to exist
convincingly in the world, even before its detailed interior is built.
