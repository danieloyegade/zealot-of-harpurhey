EASTERN BLOC + FLOCK / FLŌK, STEVENSON SQUARE, MANCHESTER

Combined untextured Blender reconstruction for Zealot of Harperhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Eastern Bloc
and Flock/Flōk as one connected architectural asset, using ALL supplied
reference photographs.

These venues are physically connected in real life and should be
modelled together as one coherent corner-building asset.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

This task is GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do not create final textures, decals, logos, graffiti, awning graphics,
chalkboard writing, branding, emissive signage, people, food/drinks or
final lighting yet.

The goal is for the untextured clay model to already be recognisable
from:

the corner massing;

Eastern Bloc frontage;

Eastern Bloc awning;

large Eastern Bloc lettering;

Flock’s arched entrance;

distinctive yellow Flock canopy and door surround;

windows;

facade mouldings;

upper brick floors;

street-facing terrace relationship.

⸻

Treat the building as a single architectural system:

SUCCESS CRITERION

The untextured clay model should already be immediately recognisable as
the connected Eastern Bloc + Flock corner.

Eastern Bloc should read through:

its large raised sign;

projecting awning;

frontage and window proportions.

Flock should read through:

its distinctive arched entrance;

radial fanlight;

projecting yellow canopy;

paired doors;

corner position.

Neither venue should look like a generic pub pasted onto the same
building.

I’d also keep the red barrel as a separate prop, even though I’d
definitely place it in the default reference scene exactly where it
appears near Flock. It is one of those small things that makes the
reconstruction feel observed rather than generic.

The awning is probably the biggest thing I’d check in the first render.
It has a surprising amount of visual mass in the references. Codex could
easily make it too shallow and turn Eastern Bloc into just another
shopfront. It needs to project far enough into the pavement that you
feel the sheltered outdoor zone underneath it.

And combining them is the right call. From the wider Stevenson Square
views, the stronger game asset is not really “Eastern Bloc” or “Flock”
individually. It’s that whole corner composition: dark Eastern Bloc sign
and awning on one side, the white facade wrapping around, then that
strange bright yellow little portal of Flock on the corner. It gives the
environment much more specificity.
