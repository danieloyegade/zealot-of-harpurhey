VILLAGE BOOKS, 131 OLDHAM STREET, MANCHESTER

Untextured Blender reconstruction for Zealot of Harperhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Village Books,
131 Oldham Street, Manchester, using ALL supplied reference photographs.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

This task is GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do not create final textures, decals, logos, books, magazines, posters,
graffiti, stickers, reflections, dirt, signage graphics or lighting yet.

The goal is to reconstruct:

-   the Village Books storefront;

-   entrance;

-   large glazing;

-   surrounding black facade frame;

-   basic ground-floor interior shell;

-   enough of the upper building to make the asset architecturally
    complete;

-   the correct relationship to the neighbouring pub, The City, which is
    visible in one of the supplied references.

The untextured clay model should already read as Village Books from its
proportions and facade layout.

⸻

1.  IMPORTANT REFERENCE LIMITATION

The storefront of Village Books is clearly documented.

The upper floors directly above it are not fully shown in dedicated
close-up references.

However, one supplied photograph of the neighbouring pub The City shows
part of the upper structure directly beside Village Books.

Use this image to infer the upper mass of the Village Books building.

Do NOT invent elaborate unsupported architecture.

Where the evidence is uncertain:

-   use the simplest plausible continuation;

-   match the visible neighbouring building height;

-   match the street rhythm;

-   maintain realistic Manchester commercial-building proportions;

-   avoid ornamental detail unless visible.

The storefront must be much more accurate than the inferred upper
facade.

⸻
