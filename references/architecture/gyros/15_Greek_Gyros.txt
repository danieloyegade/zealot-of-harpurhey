GREEK GYROS, DEANSGATE, MANCHESTER

Untextured game-ready food stand asset for Zealot of Harpurhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of the Greek Gyros
food stand in Deansgate, Manchester, using ALL supplied reference
images.

This asset is for Zealot of Harpurhey, a Three.js game.

This is a relatively small environmental asset compared with the larger
buildings, but it should still feel specific to the real stand rather
than like a generic food kiosk.

The defining visual features are:

-   rectangular freestanding kiosk form;

-   large blue GREEK GYROS fascia across the top;

-   Greek flag motifs at both ends of the fascia;

-   open serving frontage;

-   horizontal secondary strip beneath the main sign;

-   stainless-steel serving counter;

-   diamond-patterned metal cladding;

-   lower blue front panel;

-   menu/sign panels inside;

-   side wall/openings;

-   flat projecting roof/canopy;

-   bright interior work area.

This pass is primarily GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do not create final photographic textures, food imagery, logos, dirt,
stickers, menus or detailed kitchen clutter yet.
