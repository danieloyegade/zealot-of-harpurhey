RENAE

RENEE, 45-47 THOMAS STREET, NORTHERN QUARTER, MANCHESTER

Untextured Blender reconstruction for Zealot of Harpurhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Renee, 45-47
Thomas Street, Northern Quarter, Manchester, using ALL supplied
reference images.

This asset is for Zealot of Harpurhey, a Three.js game.

This task is GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do not create final textures, decals, logos, graffiti, posters, emissive
materials or lighting yet.

The goal is to reconstruct the building exterior and the ground-floor
Renee interior accurately enough that the model is recognisable in plain
clay materials.

⸻

1.  IMPORTANT TEMPORAL REFERENCE RULE

The supplied images show the building at more than one stage.

Some exterior photographs were taken before the Renee signage had been
installed.

For example, the image showing “45-47 THOMAS STREET” on the black fascia
is still the correct architectural location and facade for Renee, but
the final game asset should represent the Renee-era version of the
building.

Therefore:

ARCHITECTURE AND PROPORTIONS should be derived from all exterior
references, including the earlier pre-signage photographs.

FINAL SIGNAGE POSITION AND BRANDING LAYOUT should be derived from the
photographs that actually show Renee branding.

At this geometry stage, do not create the actual logo as a texture or
mesh unless a physical sign structure projects from the facade.

Reserve clean surfaces for the later Renee signage.

10. ACOUSTIC CEILING

The ceiling is a major architectural feature.

The references show:

dark exposed ceiling;

timber-toned perforated acoustic panels;

long rectangular ceiling rafts;

beams between panels;

pendant globe lights.

Model the large acoustic panels as geometry.

However, do not model every perforation individually.

Use clean panel geometry.

The small holes will later be represented with normal/opacity/material
detail.

Reconstruct:

panel dimensions;

panel spacing;

suspension depth;

dark gaps between panels;

relationship to structural beams.

This ceiling should produce believable parallax when the player walks
underneath it.

The nighttime red lighting is a defining part of the location’s
identity.

Do not lose this in later stages.
