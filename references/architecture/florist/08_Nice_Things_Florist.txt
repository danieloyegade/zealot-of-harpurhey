NICE THINGS, OLDHAM STREET, MANCHESTER

Florist / plant shop architectural asset for Zealot of Harperhey

Create an accurate, game-ready Blender reconstruction of Nice Things on
Oldham Street, Manchester, using ALL supplied reference photographs.

This asset is for Zealot of Harperhey, a Three.js game.

Nice Things is a hero gameplay location because the player’s first
flower delivery begins here.

This task is GEOMETRY ONLY.

Do NOT create final textures, decals, logos, plants, flowers,
handwritten signs, graffiti, stickers, dirt, final lighting or final
materials yet.

The purpose of this pass is to reconstruct:

-   the Nice Things storefront;

-   the relevant historic building facade above it;

-   entrance;

-   shop glazing;

-   architectural detailing;

-   enough interior volume for later player access;

-   gameplay interaction points.

The untextured clay model should already resemble the real location
before any pink materials, branding, plants or flowers are added.

⸻

1.  IMPORTANT GAMEPLAY CONTEXT

Nice Things is the florist used for an early delivery mission.

The intended gameplay flow will eventually be approximately:

PLAYER RECEIVES FLOWER DELIVERY JOB

↓

Player travels to Nice Things.

↓

Player enters or interacts with the shop.

↓

Player receives/picks up flowers.

↓

Flowers become a carried delivery item.

↓

Player delivers them to the recipient.

Do NOT implement this logic in Blender.

However, the architecture must support:

-   obvious entrance;

-   player navigation;

-   interaction near the counter;

-   flower pickup point;

-   shopkeeper NPC position;

-   future interior exploration.

Compare the clay model against every reference image.

Check:

1.  Nice Things shopfront width.

2.  Shopfront height.

3.  Main display-window proportions.

4.  Door position.

5.  Pink facade area.

6.  Logo location.

7.  Ground floor to upper-floor relationship.

8.  Upper window rhythm.

9.  Historic stone vertical bays.

10. Cornice positions.

11. Central Buildings arch scale if included.

12. Total building height.

13. Interior scale.

14. Player circulation.

15. Sightline from pavement into shop.

Look particularly for:

-   oversized shopfront;

-   incorrect upper-storey heights;

-   overly chunky window frames;

-   toy-like stonework;

-   generic florist architecture;

-   perfectly flat historic facade;

-   a shop interior that is visibly too shallow.
